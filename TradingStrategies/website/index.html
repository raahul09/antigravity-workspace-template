Risk Engine

Built-in Circuit Breakers Protect Your Capital

GrootTrade's risk management engine runs 7 independent safety checks before every trade execution.

Position Sizing Engine

Fixed lot or percentage-risk mode with automatic lot normalization to your broker's step size and volume limits.

Daily Drawdown Guard

Tracks equity high-water mark. Rejects new trades if daily drawdown exceeds your configured limit (e.g., 5%).

Max Concurrent Trades

Limits simultaneous open positions. Prevents over-exposure during volatile sessions or rapid signal bursts.

Entry Slippage Guard

Rejects signals if market price has moved too far from the signal's entry price. Configurable pip threshold.

Channel Streak Tracking

Tracks consecutive wins and losses per channel. Visual fire/warning badges in the dashboard.

Auto-Pause Circuit Breaker

Automatically pauses a channel when its loss streak hits your threshold. Prevents chasing bad signals.
